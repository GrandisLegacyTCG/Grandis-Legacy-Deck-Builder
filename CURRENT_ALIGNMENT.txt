Current locked runtime rules:
- Chain Mail is Warrior / Archer / Thief, not Rogue.
- Poison Mist remains 6 Mana.
- Finnian Rank III / Nightshade Venom: Poison duration +1 and all Attack damage +10.
- Venom Sovereign is non-Ultimate and may be tributed as a normal EXP card.
- Venom Detonation remains Ultimate and restricted to Thief / Rogue / Renegade lineage.
- Archer Marksman / Grand Ranger bonus applies only to Single Target Attack damage.
- Health Potion and other hero-related Items still require a Hero target; no-hero universal Items activate directly.
- Halfling Second Chance is optional, not automatic.
- Revived Rank II returns with 300 EXP; revived Rank III returns with 700 EXP.

Deployment direction:

- Do not treat free hosting as final production readiness.
